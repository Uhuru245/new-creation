# New Creation

A mobile-first Bible reading and community app for a reading circle: daily plans, the Bible in three public-domain translations, original Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek with word meanings, a praying Circle, and a Leader area.

- **App:** static progressive web app (this folder), installable and offline-capable for reading.
- **Server:** Google Apps Script plus a Google Sheet (`server/Code.gs` here is a sanitised copy used by the demo; the live copy holds the group's private settings).
- **Demo:** open the app with `?demo=1` to explore with sample people. Nothing is shared or saved outside your browser.
